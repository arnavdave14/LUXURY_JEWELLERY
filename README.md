# AURELIA — Haute Joaillerie & Cinematic Exhibition

A production-quality luxury jewellery interactive fashion film and digital exhibition built in React, Vite, TypeScript, GSAP, and Modern CSS.

---

## ✦ Design Aesthetics & Light Theme Architecture

- **Primary Background**: Porcelain Cashmere (`#FAF7F2`) & Warm Pearl (`#F5EFE6`)
- **Primary Typography**: Deep Regal Aubergine (`#2A1B27`)
- **Secondary & Accents**: Velvet Antique Rose (`#A85F72`), Radiant Champagne (`#E7D7C1`), Warm Gold (`#C9A86A`, `#D4AF37`)
- **Typography Stack**:
  - Display Serifs: *Bodoni Moda*, *Italiana*
  - Body & Editorial Serifs: *Cormorant Garamond*
  - Modern Sans & Metadata: *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Syne*, *Space Grotesk*

---

## ✦ Continuous Visual Canvas — 9 Cinematic Scenes

1. **Scene 01: Objects of Desire (Pinned Camera Hero)**
   - Pinned GSAP ScrollTrigger camera scene with 3D depth and subtle ambient aura lighting.
   - Off-grid jewellery composition with fine SVG measurement lines and optical axis HUD.

2. **Scene 02: Wear The Unexpected (Section-Breaking Multi-Layer Parallax)**
   - Multi-plane parallax offsets with fluid typography choreography on scroll.

3. **Scene 03: Editorial Collection Universe (Asymmetrical Floating Canvas)**
   - Freeform editorial landscape with staggered on-scroll reveals.

4. **Scene 04: Pinned Horizontal Panorama (Scale Contrast)**
   - True pinned horizontal scroll track translating vertical scroll into horizontal traversal with synchronized marquee banner.

5. **Scene 05: Scroll-Controlled Jewellery Film (300-Frame 360° Motion Study)**
   - Dedicated `<ScrollImageSequence />` component.
   - 300-frame 360° rotational scan with optical flare, facet refraction, timecode HUD, and unblocked glassmorphic card.

6. **Scene 06: Tactile Craftsmanship & Metallurgy**
   - 6-stage interactive progression: `RAW MATERIAL → THE HAND → CRAFT → FORM → LIGHT → JEWELLERY`.

7. **Scene 07: High-Fashion Campaign ("JEWELLERY")**
   - High-fashion editorial cutout model with giant background typography and gold pricing suite.

8. **Scene 08: The House & Curated Journal**
   - Heritage ateliers in Paris, Jaipur, and Milan with interactive essay reader modal.

9. **Scene 09: See You In Another Light (Cinematic Credits & Drifting Solitaire)**
   - Minimalist film credits with drifting gemstone and private dispatch registration.
